# Crown Quest: Story Continuity

## Royal History

- Rowan is the son of King Aldric and Queen Elowen. He was six at the wreck,
  eleven years before play begins, and is seventeen during the adventure.
- Morvane wrecked the royal ship and scattered Alderhaven's treasures. He took
  Rowan from the rocks. Elowen reached the shore beneath the Amber Tower.
- The tower's royal ward sheltered Elowen from Morvane but sealed her inside.
  Outsiders could neither identify her face clearly nor hear her voice.
- Aldric believed his wife and son drowned. He is genuinely ill, not dead.
- Morvane hid Rowan's identity and kept him as a servant while waiting for the
  ward to weaken. He could not command its opening: Elowen's heir had to freely
  reunite the treasures. Rowan's escape was not staged; Morvane exploited it
  and followed the storm's trail across the channel.
- Elowen identifies Rowan after the duel. At the castle Aldric publicly
  acknowledges his son. Weeks later he voluntarily abdicates before the
  council. Elowen crowns Rowan as his mother, not as his bride.

## Treasure Rules And Custody

- The chest sustains the ward, the shield protects its heir, and the mirror
  returns hostile magic. Reuniting them freely opens the tower to its heir.
- All three enter sockets. The chest stays in the tower; the shield and mirror
  are explicitly released and taken back before the duel.
- The shield is destroyed protecting Rowan. Its remaining light completes the
  ward's work. Losing that spent ward does not destroy the kingdom.
- During the following weeks, the chest funds recovery. The mirror and shield
  fragments go to the royal treasury. The realm's recovery is human work, not
  an unexplained restoration of a permanently required magical battery.

## Knowledge And Repeat Visits

- Rowan learned ordinary letters from flour sacks. He reads simple words
  slowly, including the backwards parchment. Morvane's magical alphabets are
  beyond him except for the spellbook's one accessible page.
- Corvus knows of the wreck and treasure locations, but does not identify Rowan
  as the heir. The reunion supplies his name and confirms his origins.
- The gnome has offered name bargains for thirty years, but has possessed the
  chest only since it washed into the watercourse eleven years ago.
- Before the bargain his visible name is "the gnome". Afterwards he is
  Mendharbe, standing without the chest and packing for a future move.
- After passing Rowan on the crag, Morvane occupies his locked upper
  observatory until dusk. Lower rooms remain accessible for essential
  backtracking; room descriptions and Corvus acknowledge his presence.
- The sea crossing is one-way. The main quest occurs over the day, followed
  by an explicit interval of weeks before the coronation.
